About Busy Bee

Busy Bee is a small independent translation project created out of my love for stories.

I translate novels that moved me, inspired me, or stayed in my heart for a long time.

My goal is to share these stories with readers in Russian so that more people can experience the beauty of Chinese literature.

Thank you for being here. You are the reason Busy Bee continues to grow. 💛
