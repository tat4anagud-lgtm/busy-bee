Synopsis

In a world where cultivation and destiny intertwine, she chose a path no one else dared to walk. A story of courage, love, and the price of ambition.

When the world despises the weak, she becomes her own strength. From a fallen clan to the heights of power, she walks a road paved with betrayal and blood.

Beside her stands a man whose smile hides deeper secrets than the night sky. Together, they will challenge fate itself.
